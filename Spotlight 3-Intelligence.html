Echo API: Intelligent Processing Engine

Technical Feature Deep Dive

How It Works

Echo API isn't just a database; it is an active intelligence engine. When you send raw text to the /logs endpoint, our system performs a real-time multi-step analysis.

1. Automatic Summarization

The engine analyzes the full text of the journal entry and generates a concise, 1-2 sentence executive summary.

Use Case: Show these summaries in your app's "History List" view so users can scan their past days quickly without opening every entry.

2. Contextual Auto-Tagging

Our AI extracts the most relevant entities, topics, and emotions from the text and converts them into an array of keyword tags.

Example: "I ran 5k today" → ["Fitness", "Running", "Cardio", "Health"]

Use Case: Implement "Clickable Pills" in your UI that let users filter their history by topic instantly.

3. Smart Categorization

The system automatically assigns the entry to one of several high-level buckets:

  • Personal: Daily reflections, memories.
  • Work: Tasks, meeting notes, career planning.
  • Ideas: Brainstorms, inventions, creative thoughts.
  • Health: Workouts, diet, mental health.
  • Event: Concerts, travel, gatherings.

4. Search Retrieval

All of these generated fields (Summary, Tags, Category) are indexed immediately. This allows your users to search their history not just for words they wrote, but for concepts related to what they wrote.
